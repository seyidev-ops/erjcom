The diagnostic

Answer honestly. Nobody is watching.

There are no wrong answers and no scoring against other people. The questions only sort your situation into one of the four points below.

The model

Four points. One leak.

A pipe does not usually burst along its length. It fails at a joint. Every remote job search runs on these same four, in this order — and when it is not producing offers, the fault is almost always at one of them.

01
Supply

Someone must see the opportunity.

The leak looks like: you cannot find real openings at all — or everything you find smells like a scam, is already closed, or was never open to applicants from here.

02
Representation

Your signal must exist and be understood.

The leak looks like: you apply and hear nothing. Your CV, LinkedIn and portfolio either do not exist, cannot be read by the software that screens them, or do not say what you can actually do.

03
Aim

The signal must reach the right target.

The leak looks like: you are applying — a lot — but at the wrong roles, the wrong companies, or into portals where the application dies. Volume without direction.

04
Conversion

Interest must become value.

The leak looks like: replies come, even interviews come — but offers do not. Or the offer arrives and the money is wrong because nobody taught you to negotiate it.

Fix one at a time. The most common mistake is trying to repair all four at once and finishing none of them. Your diagnostic above names the one to start with.
